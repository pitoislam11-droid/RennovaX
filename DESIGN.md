# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Scene

Homeowners open Rennova on a phone in a bright kitchen or living room — calm daylight, not a construction site. The surface stays light, airy, and confident.

## Color strategy

**Restrained:** warm off-white canvas (`#F7F4EC` / paper `#FFFDF8`), charcoal ink (`#1D1D1B`), muted secondary (`#6F6C63`). **Rennova yellow (`#FFD21C`)** is the single interactive/brand accent. Red (`#D84B3E`) only for selective brand moments and true destructive/error — never as a second primary.

## Type

Avenir Next family already in tokens — demi for display, medium for labels, regular for body. Large homeowner display (~32–36) for the primary ask; body ~15–17. No Inter/Roboto defaults. No eyebrow kickers above headings.

## Motion

Occasional only: home first paint uses short FadeInDown (~400ms, ease-out). Press feedback via `active:scale`. Tab switches: platform default (no slide). Reduced motion: skip staggered entrances.

## Components

Rounded paper surfaces, soft offset shadows, 44pt+ targets, yellow primary CTAs, charcoal secondary. Prefer generated choice UI over chat bubbles. Sheets for focused tasks later.

## Anti-patterns (project)

No hazard-tape yellow/red fields. No nested cards. No grey dashboard chrome. No giant forms. No fake live material prices.
